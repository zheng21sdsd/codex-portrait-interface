# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim for a welcoming, respectful project where contributors can participate without harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, patient, and specific.
- Respect differing experience levels and viewpoints.
- Focus critique on code, evidence, and impact.
- Protect other people's privacy and intellectual property.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, hateful or sexualized language, doxxing, publishing private images or data, deliberate disruption, and bad-faith security disclosure are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's maintainers. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible. Maintainers may remove content, warn contributors, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action.
